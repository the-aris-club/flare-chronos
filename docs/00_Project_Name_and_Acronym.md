# 00. Project Name, Brand Identity & Acronym

**Project:** **Flare-Chronos**

**Acronym (full name):** *Fire Long-term Archive Resolution & Cross-sensor Harmonization for Operational Near-real-time Observation System*

## Official Logo Symbolism

- **Multi-Band Thermal Flame**: Represents the $4\,\mu\text{m}$ mid-infrared radiance signal ($\text{FRP}$) harmonized across the two sensor generations, MODIS (`1 km`) and VIIRS (`375 m`).
- **Hexagonal Global Grid Base (Uber H3)**: The blue and orange hexagonal blocks beneath the flame represent the allocation of the scan-swath elliptical area weight ($\Delta S(\theta)\times\Delta T(\theta)$) onto the dual **Uber H3 Resolution 7 & 8** hexagonal grid.
- **Polar satellite orbit & `26 YEARS` ribbon**: Represents the continuous 26-year observation record (`2000–2026+`) that links data from 5 satellites (`Terra`, `Aqua`, `Suomi-NPP`, `NOAA-20`, `NOAA-21`) into a **26-Year Harmonized Burning Activity Calendar**.

## Meaning of the Name

- **FLARE** — evokes the image of a flame: the fire signal that the system detects, harmonizes and monitors.
- **CHRONOS** — emphasizes the core element of the brief, the *Burning Activity Calendar*, across more than 25 years (2000–2026+).
- The system links and harmonizes data from **NASA FIRMS** (Fire Information for Resource Management System) — both the historical archive and the near-real-time (NRT) stream — into a single, physically calibrated, sensor-independent record.

## Reusable One-Liner (Reports / Presentations / Proposals)

> **Flare-Chronos** — *Fire Long-term Archive Resolution & Cross-sensor Harmonization for Operational Near-real-time Observation System*: a 26-year, cross-sensor (MODIS + VIIRS) harmonized Burning Activity Calendar built on NASA FIRMS data.
